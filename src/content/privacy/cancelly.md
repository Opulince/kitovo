## Information Cancelly collects

### Your account

- **Email address.** Used to sign you in (with a six-digit code sent to your email, instead of a password) and to send you reminders.
- **Time zone.** Used so that dates and reminders are worked out in your local time.
- **Basic settings,** such as your language and currency preferences and your reminder settings.

### Your trials

For each trial you add, Cancelly stores its name and the date it turns into a charge, plus anything optional you choose to add, such as the price, a link to cancel, or a short note. It also stores the trial’s status (for example, kept or cancelled) and a history of changes to it, such as when you edited its date.

If you mark a trial as cancelled and it has a price, Cancelly records that amount so it can show you how much you avoided paying.

### Emails you forward

Cancelly gives you a personal forwarding address. When you forward a message to it:

- the message is read automatically to find the service’s name, the date the trial turns into a charge and, where present, the price and a link to cancel;
- Cancelly keeps those details, the short phrase the date was read from (so you can check it was read correctly) and the sending domain (for example, “example.com”);
- Cancelly does **not** keep the body of the message, its subject line or the sender’s email address.

Forwarded messages are received for us by an inbound email provider (see [Service providers](#service-providers)).

### Reminder delivery

To make sure your reminders arrive, Cancelly keeps a record of the reminders scheduled and sent for each trial, and of the delivery reports our email provider sends back (for example, whether a message was delivered or bounced). If reminders to your address keep bouncing, or you mark them as spam, Cancelly stops sending reminders to that address.

We also keep a short log of a few important account events, such as replacing your forwarding address or changing your plan.

### What Cancelly does not collect

Cancelly never asks for your bank login or card numbers, does not have access to your email inbox, and does not ask for your contacts or your location.

## How we use your information

- **To run Cancelly:** storing your trials, working out when to remind you, sending reminders and reading the emails you forward.
- **To sign you in** and keep your account secure.
- **To keep reminders reliable:** checking that reminders are delivered and fixing problems when they aren’t.
- **To help you** when you contact support.

We do not sell your personal data.

## Service providers

Cancelly relies on a small number of providers that process data on our behalf, only to provide the service:

- **Supabase:** database and sign-in. Stores your account and trial data.
- **Vercel:** runs the server code that schedules reminders and processes forwarded emails.
- **Resend:** sends reminder emails.
- **Postmark:** receives the emails you forward to Cancelly.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

## How long we keep it

- **Account and trial data** is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and everything attached to it, including your trials, reminder history and forwarding address, is deleted from our database.
- **Forwarded emails** are not stored by Cancelly. Only the details read from them are kept, and those are deleted with the trial or with your account.
- **Support emails** are kept as long as we need them to help you.

## Your choices and rights

- **Edit or delete any trial** from inside the app.
- **Delete your account** and everything attached to it from inside the app, at any time.
- **Ask us** for a copy of your data, to correct it, or anything else about how it’s used, by emailing [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}).

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We will respond to requests within the time the law requires.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only read and change its own data, and that rule is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app. Your forwarding address works like a password for adding trials: if you think someone else knows it, email us and we will replace it.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and collect as little as possible in the first place.
