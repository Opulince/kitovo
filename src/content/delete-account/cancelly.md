## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes, immediately:

- **Your account,** including your email address.
- **Every trial you saved:** names, prices, dates, cancellation links and notes.
- **Your scheduled reminders.**
- **Your savings history.**
- **Your profile settings,** such as your time zone and reminder preferences.
- **Your personal forwarding address.**
- **Related logs,** such as reminder delivery records and account activity.

**This cannot be undone.** Once your account is deleted, we cannot recover any of it. You can sign up again with the same email address, but you will start with an empty account.

If you have a Cancelly Premium subscription through Google Play, deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in the Play Store first, under **Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions**.

## What is kept

Nothing about your account is kept in our database after it is deleted. A few copies exist outside it, and we want to be clear about them:

- **Emails already in your inbox.** Reminder emails we already sent you stay in your inbox until you delete them.
- **Our email provider’s delivery logs.** Resend, which sends our reminder emails, keeps its own logs of the emails it delivered, such as the address and the time it was sent, for a limited period before deleting them.
- **Emails you forwarded to Cancelly.** Postmark, which receives them for us, may keep its own copy for a limited period before deleting it.
- **Backups.** Our database runs on Supabase’s free plan, which gives us no backups we can restore or download. Supabase may still keep its own internal daily backups for up to 7 days, so any copy of your data in them is gone within 7 days of deletion.
- **Emails you send us,** including a deletion request, are kept in our inbox only as long as we need them to handle your request.
- **Google Play purchase records.** If you bought Cancelly Premium, Google keeps its own record of the purchase under Google’s privacy policy.

If you also joined the Cancelly waitlist on our website, that sign-up is separate from your account. Email us and we will remove it too.
