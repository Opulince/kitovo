This policy explains how {{entityName}} (“Kitovo”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit this website. It does not cover our apps: each app has its own privacy policy, listed at the end of this page.

## What this website collects

### When you browse

This website is a set of static pages. It does not set cookies, run analytics, show ads, or load fonts, scripts or images from third-party services. Everything is served from the same place as the pages themselves.

Like every website, it is delivered by a hosting provider ({{hostingProvider}}). To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, the hosting provider processes technical information that your browser sends with every request, such as your IP address, browser type and the address of the page requested. We do not use this information to identify you or to build a profile of you.

### When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it to read and reply to your message and to help with whatever you asked about.

## How we use information

- To deliver this website and keep it secure.
- To reply to messages you send us.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

We don’t share information from this website, except:

- with our hosting provider, which processes request information on our behalf to run the site;
- with the provider that hosts our email inbox, which stores messages you send us;
- when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as we need them to deal with your message and any follow-up. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, email [{{generalEmail}}](mailto:{{generalEmail}}). We will respond within the time the law requires.

## Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
